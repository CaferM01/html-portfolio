The Best Movies According to Cafer

My Top 3 Movies

The Dark Knight

Christopher Nolan's 2008 superhero film is a masterpiece of modern cinema, combining intense action with deep character development.

Goodfellas

Martin Scorsese's 1990 crime film is a gripping portrayal of the rise and fall of mob associate Henry Hill, featuring unforgettable performances and a compelling narrative.

Heat

Michael Mann's 1995 crime thriller is a tense and stylish exploration of the cat-and-mouse game between a master thief and a dedicated detective, highlighted by the legendary on-screen pairing of Al Pacino and Robert De Niro.
